Consolidated guidance from Chapters 3--7 and the measured estimator
comparisons (\code{docs/empirical\_acceleration\_estimation.md}). The
method choice comes down to \textbf{two rules}, and the Python surface
keeps no method that lost in each measured scenario (the collocation facades and the legacy
IRGN multi-sensor family are gone entirely):

\begin{center}\small
\begin{tabular}{@{}lll@{}}
\toprule
Use case & Method & Key parameters \\
\midrule
Sub-meter orbit (POE ref) & RK45, GCRS, deg 70 & correct ITRF; EOP loaded \\
Long arc ($>$6 h) & YOSHIDA8 & \code{symplectic\_step\_size}$\sim$30 \\
Clean POS / VEL / ACC EA & \code{estimate\_ea\_bspline} + typed samples & GN default-ON (ACC ignores it) \\
Noisy POS EA & B-spline + \code{cfg.set\_cv()} & GN on (default) \\
Fusion (radar/optics/fix) or noisy POS & \code{estimate\_ea\_smoother} & GM-RTS; $\ge$2 radars; +camera \\
\bottomrule
\end{tabular}
\end{center}

\medskip
The modality is the sample \emph{type} ---
\code{PositionSamples} / \code{VelocitySamples} / \code{AccelSamples} ---
so mixing them up is an error at the call, not a silent misfit.
Measured headlines: Sentinel 1-hour ${\sim}0.18\,$m; clean-glide EA
${\sim}7\,$mm POS+GN, ${\sim}0.09\,$m VEL, ${\sim}8\,$mm ACC; noisy-POS +
hv-block CV ${\sim}0.2\,$m; noisy-VEL ${\sim}0.4$--$0.5\,$m; ballistic
control ${\approx}0\,$m; 2-radar
fusion ${\sim}6\,$m (geometry floor), ${\sim}0.3\,$m with a $50\,\mu$rad
camera. \textbf{Scale reference:} EA=0 on the forced glide is
${\sim}35\,$km off --- the denominator for every EA RMSE in Chapters
4--5. Dense single-modality data $\rightarrow$
\code{estimate\_ea\_bspline} with GN left ON (the old ``GN destabilizes
VEL'' rule was falsified and deleted, 2026-07); fusion or noisy positions
$\rightarrow$ \code{estimate\_ea\_smoother}. Forces are presence-as-switch
in both languages (\code{srp=SrpConfig(...)}, \code{drag\_env=...}; both
default OFF) --- set them deliberately.

\medskip
\noindent\textbf{Each parameter traces to a result in Mathematical
Foundations:} knot spacing $\leftarrow$ spline bias bound
\eqref{eq:knot} (Theorem~\ref{thm:spline}); $\lambda$ grid $\leftarrow$
bias--variance scaling \cite{wahba1990}; IRGN schedule $\leftarrow$
continuation in the enlarged ball (Proposition~\ref{prop:basin},
\cite{bakushinskii1992}); hv-block/gap $\leftarrow$ consistency under
chaotic dependence (Proposition~\ref{prop:cv}, \cite{racine2000});
``$\ge2$ radars / add a camera'' $\leftarrow$ Cramér--Rao impossibility
(Theorem~\ref{thm:cr}). Under bounded EA a positive-radius basin
certificate exists and grows with $\lambda$ (Lemma~\ref{lem:curv},
Proposition~\ref{prop:basin}) but is conservative and not global;
parameters minimize the provably consistent CV risk.
