For noisy data without truth, hyperparameters are chosen by hv-block
cross-validation: \code{cfg.set\_cv()}. We add Gaussian noise (POS 1.0\,m,
VEL 0.25\,m/s, ACC 0.1\,m/s$^2$) and run each \{obs, rate\} cell. GN
(consistent-objective regularized, 2026-07) is default-ON and benefits POS
and VEL alike; the ACC facade ignores it (circular).

The solver is iteratively-regularized Gauss--Newton~\eqref{eq:irgn}
(\cite{bakushinskii1992}); the decreasing $\lambda_k$ is the continuation
keeping the iterate in the enlarged basin (Proposition~\ref{prop:basin}).
hv-block CV is provably consistent here (Proposition~\ref{prop:cv},
\cite{racine2000}); plain $K$-fold is not. See Mathematical Foundations.

\pitfall{An earlier edition measured noisy VEL diverging outright
(${\sim}29\,$km!) with bare public-API defaults. That was real, and it
was \emph{fixed} in 2026-07: the arc-length auto-clamp removed the
line-search freeze on short arcs, and regularized GN is now default-ON
for VEL. With current defaults noisy VEL recovers ${\sim}0.4$--$0.5\,$m
--- no research harness needed. The remaining honest limit is ACC at
$\sigma=0.1\,$m/s$^2$ (${\sim}15\,$m at 100\,Hz, ${\sim}7\,$m at
200\,Hz): that $\sigma$ is artificially
large for an accelerometer (realistic $10^{-6}$--$10^{-2}\,$m/s$^2$,
where sub-meter returns), and double-integrating acceleration noise costs
the most position information. For scale, the no-EA baseline is
${\sim}35\,$km (Chapter 4). POS + GN + hv-block CV remains the most
robust noisy path; higher sample rate helps every modality.}
